      One Press

      Press once. Know in seconds.

      On a call where someone says you are under arrest, in a chat that feels off, at a payment you don't trust: press the button on the side of your phone. Red Thread reads the screen and tells you what to do, without you leaving the call.

      1. Your press link

      Only your phone uses it. Anyone with it can send presses as you, so keep it to yourself.

      2. Put it on your iPhone

      Open the Shortcuts app, tap +, and add these actions in this order.

      1. Take Screenshot
      2. Get Contents of URL. Paste your link as the URL. Tap the arrow: Method POST, Request Body File, File Screenshot.
      3. Get Dictionary Value: Value for notice in Contents of URL. Then Show Notification with Dictionary Value. Turn Play Sound off.
      4. To text family on a scam: Get Dictionary Value for alert. If it has any value, Send Message with it to the person you choose. Tap the arrow and turn Show When Run off.
      5. For the official-source check: Get Dictionary Value for later. If it has any value, Get Contents of URL with it, then Show Notification with Contents of URL.

      Name it Red Thread. Then Settings, Action Button, Shortcut, Red Thread. On older iPhones use Back Tap in Accessibility, Touch. Steps 4 and 5 are optional.
