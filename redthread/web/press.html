One Press

Press once. Know in seconds.

On a call where someone says you are under arrest, in a chat that feels off, at a bill you don't trust: press the button on the side of your phone. Red Thread reads the screen and tells you what to do, without you leaving the call.

1. Your press link

Only your phone uses it. Anyone with it can send presses as you, so keep it to yourself.

2. Put it on your iPhone

Open the Shortcuts app, tap +, and add these three actions in this order.

  1. Take Screenshot
  2. Get Contents of URL. Paste your link as the URL. Tap the arrow: Method POST, Request Body File, File Screenshot.
  3. Show Notification with Contents of URL. Turn Play Sound off.

Name it Red Thread. Then Settings, Action Button, Shortcut, Red Thread. On older iPhones use Back Tap in Accessibility, Touch.
