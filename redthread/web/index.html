How it works

Five Meta models.
Code decides.

Each model has one job. Models write words. Our code supplies the facts, so a warning never depends on a model's guess.

  1. Muse Spark 1.3Reads the screen in about 5 seconds: a call, a chat or a payment, who is calling, their exact words.
  2. Our codeDecides the warning from fixed rules and quotes checked word for word. Remembers your earlier presses.
  3. Muse Wonder 1.0Searches the web on the scam's claim while you read the first notice.
  4. Our codeKeeps only sentences that cite a government site.
  5. Voice Transcribe 1.0Hears what happened, in English, Hindi or Tamil.
  6. Our codeFills the cyber crime form, and drops any number you never said.
  7. Muse Image 1.0Makes fake scam screens that attack our checks in the test lab.
  8. SAM 3.1Finds the QR code on a restaurant bill.

Privacy

Read once.
Never saved.

  • Your screenshot is read once.It goes to Meta's Model API to be read, and Red Thread never saves it. Codes and card numbers are removed before anything is kept.
  • Everything else is gone in a day.The warning, the time and the scam number are kept only for your case, then deleted after 24 hours, or at once with Delete everything.
  • Your family text is yours.It goes from your own phone, so we never see who you send it to.

What we never do

Your date is not a suspect.

  • We never listen to your date.Red Thread hears you, and the other person only on a call they agreed to. A scam call you put on speaker is heard live and never recorded.
  • We never judge a face.No model looks at faces or says a photo is fake. We check the story around it.
  • We never call anyone a scammer.We show what doesn't hold together, with the evidence, and you decide.
