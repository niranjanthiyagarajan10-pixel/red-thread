# Submission text

## Name

Red Thread

## One line

Press your phone's side button during a scam call and know what to do in seconds, without leaving the call.

## Description

In India, "digital arrest" scams keep people on video calls for hours. Callers pose as police or CBI and say not to hang up or tell anyone, so victims can't step out to check. Dating scams follow the same path in chat.

Red Thread works where the scam is happening. One press of the phone's side button sends a screenshot. Muse Spark 1.3 reads it in about 5 seconds, and our code decides the warning from fixed rules and quotes checked word for word: "This is a scam. You can hang up." At the same moment the phone texts someone you trust, from your own number, which breaks the isolation the scam depends on. Seconds later Muse Wonder 1.0 checks the scam's claim on the web, and a second notice says what the government says, with only official sources kept. Red Thread remembers: a payment screen right after a scam call gets "Stop. Do not pay."

After the call, your case is ready: the words to say on the 1930 helpline with the number and time filled in, a first-hour timer, and the cyber crime form filled from your own voice in English, Hindi or Tamil through Voice Transcribe 1.0. Muse Image 1.0 makes the fake scam screens our test lab attacks the system with.

Models write words, code supplies the facts. Screens are read once and never saved, and everything else is deleted after 24 hours or with one tap.

## Built with

Meta Model API: Muse Spark 1.3, Muse Wonder 1.0, Voice Transcribe 1.0, Muse Image 1.0, SAM 3.1. Python standard library server, plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript.
